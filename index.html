Malaria Vaccine Research: What You Need to Know

A closer look at the research, the public-health challenge, and what happens next.

By Monty Hamon · Updated 26 October 2026
Image caption:UNICEF Ethiopia / Demissew Bizuwerk.

Overview Malaria remains a major global health challenge, but advances in vaccine development are creating new opportunities to protect vulnerable communities. Researchers and public health organisations are working to expand access to malaria vaccines, particularly in regions where the disease causes the greatest burden of illness. These efforts could help reduce severe cases and deaths, especially among young children, while supporting existing prevention methods such as mosquito nets and other control measures.
